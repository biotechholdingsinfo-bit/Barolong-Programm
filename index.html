Governance, Land, Economic Development, and Institutional Accountability

The Five-Institution Model

  • Royal House of Barolong: Custodian of Barolong identity, customary authority, unity, heritage and long-term vision [1].
  • Barolong Governance Council: Policy, customary governance and accountability institution [1].
  • Barolong National Development Trust: Long-term holding and protection of collective wealth for the benefit of the Barolong community [1].
  • Barolong Holdings: The commercial engine operating on professional commercial principles [1].
  • Barolong Development Agency: Implementation arm for social and economic transformation [1].
